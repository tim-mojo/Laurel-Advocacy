Our Approach

Laurel Advocacy meets people where they are and walks with them toward stability, through three connected pillars of care.

Emergency Services

24/7 crisis response, safety planning, and emergency shelter for individuals and families in immediate need.

Housing

Transitional and rapid re-housing programs that move families from crisis into a stable home of their own.

Self-Sufficiency

Job readiness, financial coaching, and life-skills programs that help families build lasting independence.
