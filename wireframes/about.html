Who We Are

About Laurel Advocacy

For 45 years, we've stood beside families and individuals in our community facing crisis — offering shelter, services, and a path toward self-sufficiency.

Mission & Vision

Our Mission

Laurel Advocacy exists to support neighbors in crisis with emergency services, safe housing, and the tools for lasting self-sufficiency — delivered with dignity, and free of judgment.

Our Vision

We envision a community where every family has a safe place to sleep, a path out of crisis, and the ongoing support needed to thrive on their own terms.

Our History

  1. 1980 — Founded

    A small group of neighbors opened Laurel Advocacy's doors as an emergency shelter serving a handful of families each month.

  2. 1998 — Program Expansion

    We launched our Self-Sufficiency Program, adding job readiness and financial coaching alongside emergency shelter.

  3. 2010 — New Facility

    We opened our current facility, more than doubling our shelter capacity and adding on-site case management space.

  4. Today

    We serve over 1,200 families a year across emergency services, housing, and self-sufficiency programs — and we're still growing.
