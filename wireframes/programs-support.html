How We Help

Programs & Support

Whatever crisis brought you here, our team is ready to help — with confidential, no-cost services and a plan built around your needs.

Confidential Support

Everything you share with our team stays private. You're always in control of what's shared and with whom.

No-Cost Services

Every program on this page is free to the people we serve, regardless of income or immigration status.

Judgment-Free Care

Our staff meet you where you are, without judgment, and help you decide what support looks like for you.

Emergency Services

When crisis hits without warning, our team is here around the clock. We provide immediate safety planning, a 24-hour crisis line, and emergency shelter for individuals and families with nowhere else to turn.

  • 24/7 crisis line and safety planning
  • Emergency shelter placement
  • Immediate needs support (food, clothing, transportation)

Housing Services

We help families move from crisis into a stable home through transitional housing, rapid re-housing assistance, and one-on-one housing navigation with a dedicated case manager.

  • Transitional and rapid re-housing programs
  • Housing navigation and landlord partnerships
  • Rental and deposit assistance
Learn More
[IMAGE 4:3 — Family moving into a new apartment]

Self-Sufficiency Program

Stability lasts when it's built on a foundation of skills and confidence. Our Self-Sufficiency Program pairs clients with a coach for job readiness training, financial literacy classes, and practical life-skills workshops.

  • Job readiness training and resume support
  • Financial literacy and budgeting classes
  • One-on-one life-skills coaching
Learn More
[IMAGE 4:3 — Job readiness workshop in progress]

Integrated Services

Crisis rarely shows up alone. Our case managers connect clients to healthcare, legal aid, childcare, and other community resources — coordinating care so nothing falls through the cracks.

  • Healthcare and mental health referrals
  • Legal aid partnerships
  • Childcare and family resource coordination
Learn More
[IMAGE 4:3 — Case manager reviewing a care plan with a client]

Specialty Programs

We offer programs tailored to the specific circumstances our neighbors face, including support groups for teens, services for older adults, and dedicated advocacy for survivors seeking safety.

  • Teen support and mentorship groups
  • Services for older adults
  • Dedicated survivor advocacy
