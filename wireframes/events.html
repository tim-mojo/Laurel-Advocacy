Signature Events

Annual Gala for Hope

Our flagship fundraiser brings together 300+ community members, local businesses, and program alumni for an evening of dinner, stories, and a live fundraising appeal. Proceeds directly fund a full year of emergency shelter operations.

  • Keynote from a program graduate
  • Silent auction with items donated by local businesses
  • Held every November at Grand Hall Event Center
[IMAGE 4:3 — Annual Gala for Hope ballroom setup]

Community 5K Walk for Housing

Every October, hundreds of neighbors lace up for a 5K walk through Riverside Park to raise awareness and funds for our Housing Services program. Teams, families, and first-time walkers are all welcome.

  • Free registration for program clients and their families
  • Team fundraising pages and matching-gift challenges
  • Held every October at Riverside Park
