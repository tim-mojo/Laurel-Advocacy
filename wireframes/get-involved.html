Get Involved

Get Involved

There are as many ways to support our neighbors as there are neighbors who need us. Give, volunteer, or help collect what our programs need most.

Give

Every gift — of any size, given once or every month — goes directly toward shelter, meals, and services for families in crisis. Financial gifts are the fastest way to meet an urgent need.

Make a One-Time or Monthly Gift

Monthly giving helps us plan ahead and respond quickly when a family needs us most.

Vehicle Donations

Donate your car, truck, van, or boat — running or not. We partner with a local vehicle donation program that handles pickup and paperwork, and the proceeds go straight to our programs.

[IMAGE 4:3 — Donated vehicle being picked up]

Volunteer

Our volunteers sort donations, staff events, mentor teens, and provide childcare during support groups. No experience is required — just a few hours and a willingness to help.

Sign Up to Volunteer
[IMAGE 4:3 — Volunteers sorting donation boxes]

Host a Collection

Rally your school, workplace, or faith community to collect the items our programs need most — from diapers and hygiene kits to winter coats and gift cards.

Start a Drive
