Help in a Crisis

When money runs short, start here. We help with food, rent, bills and more.

Visit Our Crisis Center

Come to 311 Laurel Avenue in Laurel. A case manager will sit down with you. You will talk about what you need and what you owe. Then you will make a plan together.

Walk in Monday to Thursday

You do not need an appointment.

Bring your ID

Bring a photo ID and proof of address if you have them.

Questions?

Call 301-776-0442.

Diabetes Prevention Program

The next group starts in January 2027. Call 301-776-0442 to save your spot.

Lower Your Risk of Diabetes

Prediabetes means your blood sugar is higher than normal. It is not diabetes yet. Many people can turn it around.

This program lasts one year. You meet with a group and a trained coach. You learn to eat well and move more. You set a small weight loss goal. The group helps each other stay on track.

What you get

  • Group meetings with a coach. You meet every week at first. Later you meet once a month.
  • Tips on food, exercise and stress that fit your life and budget.
  • Private weigh-ins so you can see your progress.
  • Online meetings you can join from home.

Who can join

  • Adults age 18 and older who are overweight.
  • People with prediabetes or a blood test that shows high blood sugar.
  • Women who had diabetes during a past pregnancy.
  • You cannot join if you have type 1 or type 2 diabetes.
  • You cannot join if you are pregnant now.

How to join

Call 301-776-0442 or email info@laureladvocacy.org.
