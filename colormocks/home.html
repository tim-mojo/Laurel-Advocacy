Emergency Services

When money runs short, start here. We help with food, rent, bills and more. You meet with a case manager who helps you make a plan, and we stay in touch after your visit.

  • Walk in Monday to Thursday, no appointment needed
  • Food, diapers and fresh produce from our pantry
  • Help with rent, power, heat and water
Get Help in a Crisis

LARS On the Go!

Some people cannot easily get to our office, so we go to them. We bring food and support to senior communities and other places where people gather.

  • THRIVE: grocery delivery and weekly check-ins for seniors
  • Project GROW: monthly gardening classes from April to October
  • Call 301-776-0442 or email mobile@laureladvocacy.org
See Services That Come to You

STAY

Long-term coaching for working families who already have a stable place to live. You work with a case manager to set your own goals and plan for the years ahead.

  • One-on-one coaching for 3 to 5 years
  • Meet with your case manager every 2 weeks
  • Rent help and money coaching in Anne Arundel County
Explore STAY

Permanent Supportive Housing

A home of your own for people who have been homeless for a long time and have a disability. A case manager visits you at home and stays with you for as long as you need us.

  • Apartments for 64 households in the Laurel area
  • Home visits and food delivered to your door
  • You do not have to be sober to get in, and a criminal record will not keep you out
