One-on-One Coaching for 3 to 5 Years

STAY stands for Stabilization Through Assistance for Your Tomorrow. In STAY, you meet with a case manager for 3 to 5 years. You pick your goals. Your case manager helps you reach them. We use a coaching method from our national partner, Economic Mobility Pathways (EMPath). It tracks your progress in 5 parts of your life.

We also ran a pilot program that gave monthly cash payments to 6 STAY families. Each family got a small payment every month for 5 months. Each family chose how to use the money. The families said it helped them feel more sure of themselves. It also gave them room to go after better jobs. The pilot program has ended.

What you agree to do

  • Meet with your case manager every 2 weeks.
  • Go to a life skills class once a month.
  • Set short-term and long-term goals with your case manager.
  • Be open and honest with your case manager.
  • Keep working toward your goals.

Who can join

  • People who live in our service area.
  • People who have a stable place to live.
  • People who have a job, or who are ready to take steps to get one.
  • People who can come to monthly classes and meetings every 2 weeks.
  • People who have nothing major stopping them from getting or keeping a job.

How to join

Call Tamara Cunningham-Myers at 301-776-0442. You can also email info@laureladvocacy.org.

Rent Help and Money Coaching in Anne Arundel County

This program started in 2026. It helps working families in Anne Arundel County pay part of their rent each month. Families also get coaching on money. The program is for families who earn more than the poverty line and still struggle to pay basic bills.

Local giving pays for this program. Our funders include the Anne Arundel Community Reinvestment and Repair Fund and the Laurel Race Course Impact Fund.
