THRIVE

For older adults in senior communities that work with us. Call 301-776-0442 or ask your community manager.

Grocery Delivery and Weekly Check-Ins for Seniors

THRIVE stands for Targeted Help, Resources and Inclusion for Vulnerable Elders. We bring groceries to your door. We can also check in with you each week. We help with doctor visits, benefits and other needs. We started in 2024 at Park View at Laurel. A friendly face each week can mean as much as the food.

What you get

  • Groceries delivered to your door.
  • A weekly visit, if you want one.
  • Help with benefits, doctor visits and local services.

Who can join

  • Older adults who live in a senior community that works with us.
  • People who need help getting food.
  • People who feel alone or need help finding services.

How to join

Call 301-776-0442 or email mobile@laureladvocacy.org. You can also ask your community manager to connect you with us.

Project GROW

Gardening classes run once a month from April to October. Call 301-776-0442 or email mobile@laureladvocacy.org to get on the list.

Learn to Grow Your Own Vegetables

GROW stands for Growing Resilience, Opportunity and Well-being. Learn to grow your own vegetables with an expert gardener. You work in the garden and learn in a classroom. We give you all the tools and supplies. You take home what you grow. You also get to know your neighbors.

From July 2025 to June 2026, 24 families took part. Together they grew 1,316 vegetables. Gardeners from 2025 came back to help the new ones.

What you get

  • 7 monthly classes with an expert gardener.
  • All the tools and supplies you need.
  • Tips on staying healthy at every class.
  • Fresh vegetables to take home.
  • A spot in THRIVE, our grocery delivery and check-in program for seniors.

Who can join

  • Families with low incomes in the areas we serve. We started at Park View at Laurel, a senior community.
  • You do not need any gardening experience.

How to join

Contact Bridgette Ratnam, MSW, at mobile@laureladvocacy.org or 301-776-0442. She leads the program.
